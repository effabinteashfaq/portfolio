# Portfolio design brief

> Where this brief and docs/portfolio-content.md disagree, the content doc wins (it is newer). Known overrides: AUCSS sits in Community, not Experience; the Ending has no GitHub link; unfinished writeups are hidden (no "Being written" covers); no Ghalib verse; the hero carries "aka 3FFYP4CK" under the name.

## Identity
Literary, desi, creative, with GRC and blue team at the core. Colourful and cultural, but professional. Palette taken from Effa's own mountain photo: mustard, mint, pine green, wildflower pink, deep ink blue, warm paper. Type: Cormorant Garamond (display), Karla (body), Noto Nastaliq Urdu (Urdu accents). Sections are named "Baab" (chapters) with Urdu and English titles.

Visual style: flat, cartoony illustration like the mountain hero scene, based on Effa's own photos, paintings and reference images. Never the photos used as they are, never copying another studio's art style, and no 3D or perspective effects.

## Concept: a house you walk through
- **Courtyard** (home): painted hero, intro, Now line, Beyond the Screen (photography, paintings).
- **Study** (degrees and research, mutala'a): a night scene with a jharokha window, moon and stars, jaali screens, a takht with a rilli and bolster, a glowing laptop showing a paper, inkpot and qalam, manuscripts tied with red ribbon, journals, kahwa and a brass lantern. Below: degrees as two bound volumes with seals, papers marked by status (wax seal for published, gold ribbon for awarded, ink blot for under review or in progress), and research interests.
- **Veranda**: Experience cards, then Community (Mehfil).
- **Corridor** (certifications): an illustrated wall: a big carved mirror in the centre, smaller colourful jharokhas and mirrors around it holding the certificates, empty frames stay as mirrors, hovering a frame shows details inside the big mirror, a narrow Persian rug as the footer.
- **Workshop** (projects, karkhana): a static illustrated daytime scene of a study table with a laptop showing graphs, parathas, an arched window onto the mountains, an easel with a painting and a stack of canvases. Then the toolkit (skills), Finished pieces, On the workbench (Khaaka featured, then planned tools).
- **Library** (writeups only): the illustrated reading nook. Each writeup is a book cover (coloured cover with a small motif, then tag, title and excerpt below). Posts read as normal scrolling pages styled like book pages.

## Scroll journey
One continuous scrolling page: Courtyard (hero) → Study → Veranda → Corridor → Workshop → Library → Ending → Persian rug footer. Each threshold has drifting chinar leaves and a patchwork strip from Effa's geometric wall painting; rooms rise in gently; a sticky bar highlights the current room (plus a Contact link). Writeups open as separate pages.

- Hero: the pink dupatta forest view in the soft palette of the first meadow hero, no jaali; "Now" line under the intro.
- Study and Workshop: the wide scenes, with edges fading softly into each section's background (no shaped frames, no interactivity).
- Veranda Community: activities text on the left half, a gallery wall on the right half. The wall stays roughly the height of the text beside it and never wider than half the section; frames are packed in rather than growing it, mixed evenly with breathing room.
- Corridor: landscape mirror wall that fits a laptop screen, frames spread sideways.
- Library: interactive shelf; writeup spines on the middle shelf open in a book beside the chair; book-cover grid below.
- Ending: the haveli's carved door open onto mountains at dusk with a lit lantern, beside "Thank you for walking through", "Open to GRC and SOC opportunities", email, CV and LinkedIn.

## Decisions
- Writeups are readable on the site itself. When cross-posting to Medium or LinkedIn later, set the canonical URL to the site.
- Education and Experience are separate sections, not a trail.
- Tech, logs and personality are mixed in: a footer log line, a site search styled like a Splunk query, room status lines.
- Transitions: chinar leaves drift across like an autumn breeze between rooms. Under 1 second, off for visitors who prefer reduced motion. Effa really wants this one.

## Usability rules
- A plain nav bar on every page and the CV one click away everywhere.
- Every immersive room has a "View as list" option.
- Works on a phone: wide scenes scroll by swiping, the shelf wraps, tapping works like hovering.
- Launch small: hide anything still empty; add content later by editing a file and pushing.
